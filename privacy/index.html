LithoCraft Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

LithoCraft is a geological sample description app published by Keith Daniel Rodgers ("LithoCraft", "we"). This policy explains what information the LithoCraft Commercial app and its license service collect, why, and your choices. Questions: lithocraft.support@gmail.com.

Your work stays on your computer

The rock descriptions, depths, queue, custom terms and exports you create are stored only on your computer. LithoCraft does not upload them and has no advertising or analytics tracking.

What we collect, and why

  • Device identifier. A one-way scrambled (hashed) identifier derived from your Windows installation, plus a random installation ID. Used to run the free trial, to tie a paid license seat to a computer, and to prevent license abuse. It cannot be reversed into your computer's real identifiers.
  • Computer name. The label you see in "Your Computers & Billing" (by default your computer's name), so you can tell your licensed computers apart.
  • Email address. Collected by Stripe when you buy. Used to match your purchase to your account, send one-time sign-in codes, and contact you about your license.
  • Subscription details. Plan, number of seats, billing period and status, received from Stripe so the app knows whether your license is active.
  • Agreement acceptance. The agreement version you accepted and when.
  • Technical logs. Our servers may record IP addresses and request times for security and troubleshooting.
  • Saved sign-in. After you sign in, a session token is saved on your computer, encrypted with Windows, so you stay signed in. "Sign out" removes it.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe's use of your information is described in its own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Who else handles data

We use service providers only to run the service: Stripe (payments), DigitalOcean (server hosting in the United States) and Brevo (sending sign-in code emails). We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Trial and license records are kept while your account or license is active and afterwards for as long as needed for billing, tax, fraud-prevention and legal purposes. Sign-in codes expire within minutes and sign-in sessions expire automatically. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below), except where we must keep records by law.

Your choices and rights

Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to close your account. You can remove a computer from your license in the app, cancel your subscription through "Manage billing", and sign out at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor them.

Security

Connections to our license service use HTTPS, licenses are digitally signed, and sign-in codes are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

LithoCraft is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify license holders by email.
